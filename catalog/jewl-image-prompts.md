# Jewl product photo prompts

One prompt per product in `jewl-products-mxn.csv`. Every prompt uses the same backdrop and light, so the catalog reads as one set and matches the site's arch-shaped image slots.

- **Format:** vertical 4:5, at least 2000 × 2500 px. The theme crops the top into an arch, so each piece sits in the lower two thirds.
- **Metal variants:** each prompt shows 18k yellow gold. For the white and rose gold variants, replace “18k yellow gold” with “18k white gold” or “18k rose gold” and keep everything else the same.
- **Negative prompt** (for tools that take one): text, watermark, logo, hands, model, mannequin, props, flowers, velvet box, dark background, harsh shadows, oversaturated colours, plastic or CGI look, bent or extra prongs, extra stones, warped band, blurry, dust, fingerprints

Upload the yellow-gold image as each product's main image, and attach the white and rose gold images to those variants so the metal swatches switch the photo.

## Brand images

**Hero / loading page (16:9 or 4:5):** A single round pale aquamarine solitaire ring in polished 18k yellow gold resting on shallow, crystal-clear turquoise water over pale sand, soft morning sun creating rippling caustic light across the gold, airy and luminous, sky-blue and gold palette, editorial luxury jewellery photography, no text.

**Atelier (4:5):** Close-up of a goldsmith's hands setting a small aquamarine into an 18k gold ring under a loupe, warm workbench light mixed with cool daylight from a Lisbon window, brass tools softly out of focus, documentary luxury photography, no faces, no text.
